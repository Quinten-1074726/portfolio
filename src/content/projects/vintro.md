## About the project

Vintro is an app for practising the complete job interview process. Make a CV in a few easy steps, write a motivation letter and do a job interview, with help and feedback along the way from Victoria, your personal AI job coach.

The goal of the project was to create a start-up from scratch: from finding a problem in Rotterdam to creating the solution.
