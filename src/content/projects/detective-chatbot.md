## About the project

An interactive chatbot game in which you team up with an AI detective to solve a mystery. Instead of simply giving answers, the chatbot stays in character as a detective, gives hints and steers the conversation, so you have to ask questions, gather clues and investigate the suspects yourself.

The goal was to create a more interactive and engaging AI experience than a standard chatbot.
